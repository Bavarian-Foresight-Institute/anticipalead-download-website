About AnticipaLead

AnticipaLead is a research-based, scenario-driven serious game that enables teams to explore how emerging technologies could reshape organisations and leadership, including the way decisions are made under uncertainty.

Participants enter a possible future and make decisions from within that environment. Each game combines a future scenario with selected emerging technologies. Depending on the chosen version, participants may also take on predefined leadership roles that introduce different priorities and ways of reasoning.

The same challenge can therefore be approached from strategic, ethical, analytical, technological or implementation-oriented perspectives to create productive tensions within the group. Participants are encouraged to question assumptions and consider consequences while reflecting on how leadership responsibilities may change as technologies and organisational environments evolve.

AnticipaLead provides a safe environment in which teams can rehearse decision-making before emerging developments become immediate organisational pressures. Participants explore possible consequences of technological change and encounter conflicting priorities that may require difficult leadership choices.

The game is designed for groups of 4-7 participants and can be played with or without an external moderator. Scenarios, technologies and perspectives can be selected according to the needs of the participating organisation. This makes AnticipaLead applicable across corporate and public-sector organisations as well as research, security and military contexts.

All AnticipaLead game materials are freely accessible and designed so that organisations can prepare and run sessions independently.

AnticipaLead may be used in educational and commercial organisational settings. The materials may not be resold, repackaged or distributed as a separate commercial product without permission from the authors.

“AnticipaTech” → “AnticipaLead”

AnticipaLead builds on an established collaboration between armasuisse Science and Technology and the Bavarian Foresight-Institute.

The cooperation initially centred on the development of the Scenario Game (AnticipaTech • L'Atelier des Futurs). The Scenario Game was created to make strategic foresight more accessible and experiential. Participants combine technological developments with broader trends to construct and explore alternative futures.

The Scenario Game centres on exploring which futures may emerge and how different developments could interact. AnticipaLead takes these possible futures as the starting point for examining leadership. Participants are placed inside a future context and asked what technological change could mean for organisational responsibility, decision-making and the role of leaders.

The project combines the technology foresight expertise of armasuisse/deftech, led by Dr Quentin Ladetto, with the strategic foresight and participatory-method expertise of the Bavarian Foresight-Institute. The collaboration has progressively developed from exploring possible futures toward experiencing what it could mean to lead within them.

About the project partners

armasuisse Science and Technology / deftech

deftech (Deftech • L'Atelier des Futurs) is the Technology Foresight programme of armasuisse Science and Technology, the competence centre for technology within the Swiss Federal Office for Defence Procurement.

Its work focuses on identifying emerging and potentially disruptive technologies at an early stage and investigating their possible implications for defence and security as well as organisations and society. The programme explores future applications, emerging dependencies and risks alongside the broader strategic consequences of technological change.

Through technology monitoring, experimentation and scenario-based approaches, deftech supports dialogue between experts and decision-makers. Its work helps organisations engage with technological change while developments are still emerging and different courses of action remain possible.

This anticipatory perspective forms an important foundation of AnticipaLead. Emerging technologies are understood as developments that can alter organisational structures and leadership responsibilities. They may also change human-machine relationships and the conditions under which decisions are made.

Bavarian Foresight-Institute

The Bavarian Foresight-Institute, based at Technische Hochschule Ingolstadt, conducts scientific research and applied work in strategic foresight and technology-oriented futures studies.

Its research examines how technological change interacts with wider societal and organisational developments. A central concern is how organisations can prepare for uncertain environments and retain their capacity to act as conditions change.

The Institute develops and applies a broad range of foresight approaches. These include horizon scanning and trend analysis as well as scenario planning, technology assessment, participatory foresight and serious games. A particular focus lies on translating foresight insights into formats that allow decision-makers to actively engage with possible futures.

Within AnticipaLead, this expertise informs the scenario-based structure of the game and its treatment of uncertainty. It also shapes the use of perspective-taking to challenge established assumptions. The game reflects the Institute's understanding of foresight as an organisational capability that supports the exploration of alternatives and helps identify implications before they become immediate challenges.
